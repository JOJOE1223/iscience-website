    Important scope

    Costing is not formulation.

    This calculator estimates feed production costs from user-supplied ingredient prices and inclusion rates. It does not formulate diets or assess nutritional adequacy, animal health, ingredient safety, regulatory compliance or expected animal performance. Feed formulations should be reviewed by an appropriately qualified professional.
