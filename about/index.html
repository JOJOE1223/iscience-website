About IScience

Science with a practical point of view.

IScience is an emerging Western Cape agricultural-science and technical-development business focused on useful, defensible progress.

Who we are

A partner for technical agricultural problems.

IScience works across animal nutrition, feed formulation, feed technologies, processing and technical product development. The aim is to operate as a technical processing and development partner rather than simply a commodity feed trader.

The business is being developed in phases. This means separating established capability from projects that are still being evaluated, developed or validated.

How we work

Evidence first

Claims should follow evidence, testing and validation.

Practical by design

Scientific ideas must work under real production and commercial conditions.

Quality and traceability

Consistency and controlled processes matter as much as formulation.

Develop before claiming

Work under development is described as development work, not marketed as proven products.

Current focus

Building in phases.

Current areas of focus include animal nutrition, formulation, feed technologies, processing, quality and consistency, traceability and practical agricultural innovation.

Practical feed and processing opportunities come first. More advanced research areas can be added when the underlying operation is ready to support them responsibly.
